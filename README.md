# Uber Data Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Uber booking and revenue data using Power BI to evaluate business performance, understand booking patterns, compare vehicle categories, and explore customer cancellation behavior.

The interactive dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, booking performance, vehicle utilization, customer behavior, payment preferences, and trip locations.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall revenue and booking performance.
- Analyze completed and lost bookings.
- Compare revenue contribution and performance across vehicle categories.
- Explore monthly and quarterly revenue trends.
- Understand customer cancellation patterns and their reported reasons.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Examine trip distances and popular pickup and drop-off locations.
- Identify patterns that can support operational and business decisions.

## Dashboard Pages

The dashboard consists of six interactive pages:

1. **Overview:** High-level view of revenue, bookings, trip distances, and popular locations.
2. **Vehicle Analysis:** Comparison of vehicle categories based on revenue, booking volume, distance, and ratings.
3. **Vehicle Details:** Detailed performance metrics for each vehicle category.
4. **Revenue Analysis:** Revenue trends, customer revenue contribution, average booking value, and payment methods.
5. **Rider Analysis:** Rider activity, customer cancellations, and cancellation reasons.
6. **Location Analysis:** Trip distance distribution and customer cancellation patterns.
